THE WORKSHOP / RESERVOIR HILLS / DURBAN

Built across disciplines.

Serious custom work rarely stays in one lane. Mechanical, electrical, body, paint and development decisions affect each other — so S&A's workshop is presented around the whole car.

01

Complete custom builds

For projects that need more than a single modification: the build is planned as one car, from concept and integration through finish.

CONCEPT → SHELL → INTEGRATION → FINISH
02

Engine + DSG swaps

Mechanical pairing is only part of the job. The quality lives in the integration — fitment, transmission, supporting systems, wiring and final behaviour.

ENGINE → TRANSMISSION → SYSTEMS → TEST
03

Custom wiring

The invisible layer that separates a conversion from a complete car. Wiring is treated as part of the build, not an afterthought.

PLAN → LOOM → INTEGRATE → VERIFY
04

Panel + paint

Repair, reshaping, panel preparation, colour and finish — handled with the final stance and character of the car in mind.

REPAIR → PREP → COLOUR → FINISH
05

Restoration + OEM+

Bring the car back without sanding away the reason it mattered. The objective is a tighter, cleaner version of the car's original character.

ASSESS → RESTORE → REFINE → PRESERVE
06

Performance development

Numbers become meaningful when they are documented. Build, refine and prove the package beyond the workshop floor.

BUILD → REFINE → TEST → PROVE
