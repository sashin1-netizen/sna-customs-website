One package. Multiple systems.

Mechanical integration

Engine and transmission choices affect fitment, driveline layout and the supporting hardware around them. The project is scoped as a complete system rather than an isolated part change.

Custom wiring

Electrical integration is part of the conversion story. S&A lists custom wiring as a core workshop discipline and treats it alongside mechanical work.

Development

Where a project is performance-focused, the package can move into testing and refinement rather than stopping at installation.
