THE APPROACH

Plan. Integrate. Verify.

Built into the project

Wiring is treated as part of the build architecture rather than a last-minute patch after the mechanical work is complete.

Conversion support

Engine and transmission swaps can create electrical integration requirements. S&A brings wiring into the same workshop scope where appropriate.

Whole-car thinking

For custom builds and restorations, electrical decisions are considered alongside the mechanical and finishing work that surrounds them.
